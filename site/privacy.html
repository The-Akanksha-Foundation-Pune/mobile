Privacy Policy

Capture Akanksha app · The Akanksha Foundation · Effective 22 October 2026

In short: Capture Akanksha is an internal app for Akanksha teams to plan events and share photos, videos and stories from them. We collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

The Capture Akanksha app ("the app") is operated by The Akanksha Foundation ("Akanksha", "we", "us"), Pune, India. Akanksha decides how and why the personal data described here is processed.

2. What we collect

DataWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Name, email address, Google account ID, profile picture Your Google account when you sign in To create your account, sign you in and show who uploaded content
Employee code, city, cost center, role Akanksha's staff records, or what you choose in the app To link your account to your team and decide what you can see and do
Photos, videos, audio, captions and event details you upload You To publish events, posts and stories in the app and for Akanksha's communications
Google Calendar events (read-only) Your Google Calendar, only if you choose to connect it To show your calendar events and help you add them to the app
Donor names and email addresses Entered by authorised staff To record updates shared with donors about the programmes they support
Basic technical data (for example request logs and error details) Your device and our servers To keep the app secure and fix problems

3. Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take a photo or record a video in the app.
  • Photos and media: used only to pick files you choose to upload.
  • Location (while using the app): used on your device to suggest your city. Your exact location is not sent to or stored on our servers.

You can turn any of these off in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

4. Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar, the app asks for read-only access (calendar.readonly). We use it only to show your events inside the app. We do not change your calendar, sell this data, use it for advertising, or let people read it except where needed for security or by law. You can disconnect at any time from your Profile in the app, or from your Google account permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Photos of children and other people

Content uploaded to the app may show students, families and staff. Only capture and upload images of people in line with Akanksha's consent and child-safeguarding guidelines. Uploads are reviewed before they are shared more widely, and you can ask us to remove any content (see section 9).

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help run the app, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Google: sign-in, Google Calendar (if connected), and Google Drive storage for uploaded media.
  • Cloud hosting providers: to run our servers and database (for example Amazon Web Services and Vercel).
  • Google Gemini API: only if the optional "Polish with AI" feature is turned on and you use it. Then the text you choose to polish is sent to Google to suggest improvements.

Content you upload is visible to other signed-in Akanksha users according to their role. Approved content may be used in Akanksha's communications. We may also disclose data if required by law.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while you have an account. We keep uploaded content as long as Akanksha needs it for its programmes and records, or until you ask us to delete it. If you disconnect Google Calendar, we delete the access tokens we stored for it.

8. How we protect data

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited by role, and only authorised staff can see admin and donor information. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete your account, your data or specific content you uploaded;
  • withdraw consent you gave, for example by disconnecting Google Calendar or turning off a permission.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

The app is meant for Akanksha staff and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
